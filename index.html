            CraigAds Google Calendar

            Connect your CraigAds calendar once and it stays up to date on its own.

            1. Open Google Calendar settings on a computer.
            2. On the left, under Settings for my calendars, click your CraigAds calendar.
            3. Scroll to Integrate calendar and copy the Calendar ID (or the whole Embed code).
            4. Paste it below and tap Connect.

            Showing a blank box? You need to be signed in to Google with access to this calendar in the same browser. This preview can't show it either; it appears once the planner is on GitHub. Open in Google ↗
