# Khristine's Daily Planner

A personal planner for work (CraigAds, CVS and personal projects) and life at home: today's to-dos, a calendar with next week's events, a verse for the day, a habit tracker, a focus timer and a notes page.

## Put it online with GitHub Pages

1. Sign in at github.com and click **New repository**. Name it something like `planner`, keep it **Public**, and click **Create repository**.
2. On the new repository page, click **uploading an existing file**.
3. Drag in `index.html`, `README.md` and the whole `images` folder, then click **Commit changes**.
4. Go to **Settings → Pages**. Under **Branch**, choose `main` and `/ (root)`, then click **Save**.
5. After a minute or two, your planner is live at `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/planner/`. Bookmark it, or on your phone use **Add to Home Screen**.

## Good to know

- **Your tasks are private.** Everything you type saves in your own browser only, not on GitHub. Other people who open the link see a blank planner.
- **Each browser keeps its own copy.** Use **Settings → Copy backup** on one device, then paste it into **Restore** on another to move your data. Copy a backup now and then to be safe.
- **Google Calendar:** in the Calendar tab, choose **CraigAds Google Calendar** and paste your Calendar ID (Google Calendar → Settings → your calendar → Integrate calendar). You need to be signed in to Google in the same browser to see it.
- **Adding photos of Aria:** upload new images into the `images` folder (e.g. `aria-4.webp`), then in `index.html` add the file name to the `PICS` list.
- **Verses** come from the Berean Standard Bible (public domain), one for each day of the year, with a link to read each one in the NIV.
